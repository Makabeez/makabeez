CRITICAL CC-01

The fee waiver lets one owner trade free through a second key

Where
Fee rule: each side pays max(1%, discount). A side trading 1% or more better than the close pays the gap back instead of the fee, and the key on the other side pays the 1%.
Evidence
772 of the 772 fills behind the final top 25 were on the fee-free side. 452,949,720 POLF of fees landed on other keys. Paying the fee, even perfect foresight tops out at +589; the winner scored +1,576.92.
Reproduce
Run analysis/replay_archive.py against the public archive. It reproduces 24 of the 25 official final scores to 0.0001 before it measures anything.
Fix
Charge the fee on top of the gap: fee = 1% + max(0, discount). Written as a patch with tests, and the fold stays zero-sum. Status: fix written and tested, not merged (the contest repo was archived first)

TRADING CONTEST · FLOP LABS CLOSE-1

The fee rule decided the winners

I replayed all 7,606,301 public trades with the contest's own account logic and matched 24 of the 25 official final scores to 0.0001. Two problems came out of the data.

  • A fee waiver let owners trade free through a second key: 772 of 772 top-25 fills used it
  • The official live board ranked everyone at a price the rules say "sets nothing". At one point it showed its leader at 207.76; at the payout price, about 87

I built an independent board that scores at the real price and checks every signature, and wrote a fee-rule fix with tests.
